Mathematical Formulation

Closed-Form Multiset Counting

Pieces of the same type and colour are physically and rule-wise indistinguishable. A board with two Blue Rocks does not distinguish which rock occupies which cell. Given $N^2$ cells, and $k$ surviving pieces of multiset counts $\{n_{B,R}, n_{B,P}, n_{B,S}, n_{R,R}, n_{R,P}, n_{R,S}\}$, the total distinguishable arrangements is:

Placements = ∑ [ (N²)! / ( (N² - ∑ alive)! · ∏ (alive!) ) ]

Summed over all legal survival counts $0 \le \text{alive}_i \le n_i$. Total directed game states doubles this figure ($2 \times \text{Placements}$) to account for the binary turn flag (Blue or Red to move).
