    Set up a position

    Pick a piece, then click squares to place or remove it. First move follows your settings.

    How to play

    Two players — Blue and Red — take turns moving pieces. Each starts with two rocks, two papers, two scissors.

    Movement

    • Rock steps one square in any direction — like a king.
    • Paper slides any distance straight — like a rook.
    • Scissors slides any distance diagonally — like a bishop.

    Territory

    Every square a piece lands on is painted your colour forever. Move onto an enemy piece to capture it — you take the square and remove one of their painters.

    Winning

    The game ends when the whole board is painted (or neither side can move). Whoever holds the most squares wins.
