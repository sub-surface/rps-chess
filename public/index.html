        How to play

        Two players — Blue and Red — take turns. Each starts with two rocks, two papers, two scissors.

        Movement (classic)

        • Rock steps one square any way — a king.
        • Paper slides straight — a rook.
        • Scissors slides diagonally — a bishop.

        Capturing (RPS)

        By default you may only capture a piece you beat: rock takes scissors, scissors takes paper, paper takes rock.

        Territory

        Every square a piece lands on is painted your colour. You can only stop on empty unclaimed ground or an enemy — so every move gains territory and the board fills. Most squares wins.

        Variants

        Change board size, movement, or starting layout, take up to 3 actions per turn, let sliders leave an ink trail, or switch to pure elimination — all from the home menu.
